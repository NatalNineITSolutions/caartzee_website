# How to Sell Furniture Online in 9 Simple Steps

Selling large items online presents unique challenges, particularly in shipping and visualization. Here’s how Caartzee makes it easy.

1. **Categorized Catalogs**: Organize your furniture by room or style.
2. **AR and 3D Visuals**: Let customers see how that sofa looks in their living room.
3. **Heavy-Duty Logistics**: Integration with furniture-specialist delivery partners.
4. **Detailed Specifications**: Dimensions are crucial for furniture sales.
