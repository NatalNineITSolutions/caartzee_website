# How to Start a Candle Making Business

Candles are one of the most popular handmade items sold online. This guide will take you from melting wax to your first sale on Caartzee.

1. **Define Your Niche**: Aromatherapy, decor, or eco-friendly?
2. **Quality Ingredients**: Sourcing the best wax and wicks.
3. **Brand Identity**: Logo and packaging that speaks to your audience.
4. **Pricing Strategy**: Factoring in labor and premium materials.
